# A fresh look & ignored artists

Lowkey gets a visual refresh: a redesigned player that can float over the page, glass in more places, page backgrounds and pure black, a calmer Home with shelf icons, and smoother motion on fast screens. And you can now ignore artists you don't want to hear.

## New

- **Ignored artists.** Ignore an artist from their page with the button beside Shuffle: one press skips their own songs, a second press also skips songs they only feature on, a third lets them play again. Their songs are dimmed in lists, and their artist and album cards are dimmed too.
- **Skipped as they start.** A song by an ignored artist is skipped the moment it comes up, in the direction you were going, so pressing Previous keeps going back.
- **Settings › Ignored.** Every ignored artist in one list, where you can choose their songs only or also features, or remove them. **Play songs I pick** decides whether a song you click yourself still plays.
- **Floating player.** In Settings › Layout › Player, the player can float over the page on glass, as wide as what it shows, instead of sitting docked at the bottom.
- **Custom sidebar order.** Drag any row in the sidebar to reorder its section. Drag a row onto Pinned to pin it, or out of Pinned to unpin it. Sort by › Custom order keeps the order you made.
- **Your playlists, separately.** Playlists you made can have their own section, apart from the ones you saved.
- **Paste a link.** Paste a Spotify link into search to open the playlist, album or artist directly. A song link plays the song and opens its album.
- **Page background.** Settings › Appearance › Page background: neutral grey, a faint tint of the playing cover, or see-through glass. **Pure black** turns the dark theme black for OLED screens.
- **Card size.** Settings › Layout › Card size makes Home's cards smaller or larger.
- **Hide card names.** Home's card names stay hidden until you point at a shelf, so the covers have the room. Turn it on in Settings › Layout.
- **Shelf icons.** Each Home shelf has an icon picked from its name and its title beside it. A "More like…" shelf shows that artist's picture.
- **A line for the day.** Home greets you with a short, dry line about listening, which changes with the day and the hour.
- **Links and credits.** Settings › About lists the website and its mirrors, the projects that inspired Lowkey, and the open-source projects it's built on.

## Improved

- **Player.** Progress and volume are thin lines that swell into a capsule with their value inside when you touch them. Shuffle and repeat moved to the right, next to the volume, and their tooltips say what they're set to. A new song slides in the direction you skipped.
- **Smoother on fast screens.** On macOS the app is no longer held to 60 frames per second, so animations and scrolling use the screen's full refresh rate.
- **Big libraries.** A sidebar with hundreds of items draws only the rows you can see, so it stays fast however much you've saved.
- **Changes in the sidebar glide.** Pinning, unpinning, saving or removing an item moves the rows into place instead of jumping.
- **Hero colours.** Albums, playlists and artists without a banner take their page's top colour from their own cover. An artist's banner fades into the page, with their name and bio on a glass card.
- **More glass.** Menus, tooltips and the hero's buttons are frosted glass too. In light mode the glass follows the Transparency setting more strongly, so it looks different from opaque.
- **Home shelves.** Choose which shelves show in Settings or with the menu beside Home's date. Your choice stays even when Spotify renames a shelf.
- **Cards keep their size.** Going back from Search to Home no longer shows the cards large for a moment before they shrink.
- **Shorter path.** A long path in the toolbar keeps its first step and the last three; the steps between fold into a **…** menu, so you can still jump back to any of them.
- **Back to a search.** Going back to a search brings back what you searched for and its results, even if you've cleared the search field.
- **Settings survive.** Your settings are also saved securely by the app, so they come back if the app's web storage is ever cleared.
- **Explicit badge.** The setting is renamed to make clear it only shows the E mark and hides nothing.
- **Updates.** Settings › About shows whether you're up to date, and a downloaded update installs when you restart or quit.
- **Error page.** If the interface runs into an error, it shows a page with a Reload button instead of going blank.

## Removed

- **For you / Full feed.** Home has one feed. Pick its shelves in Settings › Layout › Content instead.

# Visualizer & folders

Lowkey learns to listen: the playing marker can move with the music, and a new full-window visualizer turns the playing cover into a disc riding the sound. The sidebar gets Spotify's folders and full drag and drop, and song lists can be sorted.

## New

- **Visualizer (Beta).** The button left of Shuffle opens the playing song's cover as a disc in a wave drawn from the music: bass at the bottom, highs at the top, the whole disc pushed towards you on every kick. Click the cover to play or pause, the window's left and right sides to skip; the controls fade away while you watch. Esc or the close button leaves it.
- **Live visualizer.** In Settings › Visualizer the playing marker's five bars become a level meter of what's playing, bass to highs. **Liveliness** sets how lively it moves, **Only while focused** pauses it when you're in another app. It listens only to Lowkey's own sound and nothing leaves your Mac; macOS asks for permission the first time.
- **Visualizer settings.** Frame rate up to 240 fps on fast screens, **Fisheye** to bulge the cover into a sphere, and **Solid background** to keep it opaque over a glass page.
- **Sort songs.** Playlists, albums and Liked songs have a **Sort songs by** bar: date added, title, artist, album or duration, and a button to reverse the order. Rows glide into their new places; playing still follows Spotify's order.
- **Spotify folders.** Your Spotify folders show in the sidebar as sections of their own, right under Pinned, nested folders by their path. Create, rename, delete and reorder them; changes go straight to Spotify.
- **Move to folder.** Drag a playlist into a folder, or use **Move to folder** in its right-click menu. Drag it out again, or choose **Remove from folder**.
- **Drag songs onto playlists.** Drag a song from any list onto a playlist or Liked songs in the sidebar to add it. It lifts off as a small card and flies into the playlist's cover.
- **Create from the sidebar.** Right-click the sidebar for **Create playlist** and **Create folder**. The new one gets its name field at once, right in its row.
- **Rename in place.** Rename a playlist or folder right in the sidebar, or click a playlist's title on its page. Press Enter to save, Esc to cancel.
- **Edit description.** Click your playlist's description, or **Add description**, to edit it in place.
- **Mark changed playlists.** A small dot marks playlists that changed on Spotify since you last opened them. Turn it off in Settings › Layout.
- **Open new playlists.** A playlist you create opens right away. Turn it off in Settings › Layout to only add it to the sidebar.
- **Menu highlight.** Settings › Appearance › Colors › Menu highlight: a neutral grey highlight in menus, or the accent colour.

## Improved

- **Full sidebar control.** Any item goes to any place its section allows: into Pinned, out of Pinned to exactly where you drop it, between folders, out of a folder into your playlists. The rows make room where it lands and close up where it left. Sections it can't go into dim while you drag.
- **Instant playlists.** A playlist, album or artist you've opened before shows at once when you open it again, even after restarting the app, and refreshes in the background.
- **Right-click menu.** Items are grouped (play; rename, move, pin; remove; create), with a clearer highlight that never clashes with a cover's colour. **Delete** and **Remove** ask on themselves: the first click turns red and asks again, the second does it.
- **Made by others.** With your playlists in their own section, the others are now called **Made by others**.
- **Dragging feels native.** A dragged row lifts on frosted glass with a soft shadow, its slot left as a well. Rows you pass over no longer light up.
- **Settings sidebar.** Grouped into Essentials, Extras (Visualizer, Ignored artists) and App.
- **Denser glass.** The toolbar capsules, the floating player and menus use the denser glass, so a bright banner behind them doesn't wash them out.

## Fixed

- Menu separators that sometimes disappeared.
- "Click again to delete" cut short in menus.
- Songs dragged to the sidebar's top or bottom picking a playlist while the list scrolled.
- A pinned playlist moved to a folder from the menu that seemed to stay put.
- The page jumping and flashing white while editing a description.

## Removed

- **The + button.** The sidebar's + button is gone. Create playlists and folders from the right-click menu.

# Frosted glass & loading

The toolbar, Settings and About the artist move onto frosted glass, sidebar sections now fold away, and big playlists and artist pages open much faster.

## New

- **Glass toolbar.** The toolbar now floats on two frosted-glass capsules: the sidebar toggle, back/forward and the path on the left; search, settings and updates on the right. With the sidebar collapsed on macOS, the left capsule takes in the window buttons.
- **Glass sheets.** Settings and About the artist open on frosted glass too, a little denser than the toolbar so text stays easy to read.
- **Follows Transparency.** How see-through the glass is follows Settings › Transparency, tuned so the toolbar and sheets stay readable at any setting.
- **Collapsible sidebar sections.** Click a section's label (Playlists, Albums, Artists) to fold it; the app remembers which are folded.

## Improved

- **Faster playlists.** Big playlists open several times faster: songs now load about a hundred per request instead of one by one. A playlist of about 500 songs opens in **0.6 s** instead of 1.3 s, one with over 4,000 songs in **about 1.7 s** instead of 9.6 s.
- **Nothing left out.** Every song still loads, in the same order and with the same details. Songs a bulk request misses load one by one, so a list is never short, and a song that appears twice is loaded only once. Playback, shuffle, queue and editing work as before.
- **Playlists you've opened.** They show instantly while the app is running and refresh in the background.
- **Song versions.** In playlists a song's version is now part of its title ("Song - Radio Edit") instead of a separate small label.
- **Faster artist pages.** An artist's page shows as soon as its content is ready instead of waiting for the banner, which loads alongside. A placeholder of the same size holds the banner's place, so the page doesn't jump when the photo arrives.
- **Banners load early.** Hovering over an artist starts loading their banner, so it's often there by the time you click, and going back to an artist shows the banner you've already seen without loading it again.
- **About the artist.** The whole bio block in an artist's hero is now one button that opens the full bio, and its hover is only as wide as the text.
- **Artist banner.** The fade from the banner into the page is shorter.
- **Scrolling under the toolbar.** Content no longer fades out at the top. A soft panel veil under the toolbar hides what scrolls up behind the capsules, and the bottom fade is shorter.
- **Settings layout.** The rail is glass and the page beside it sits on its own panel, with no divider line. With Edge to edge on, the page runs to the window's edge with its rounding kept.
- **Sidebar covers.** Hovering a cover in the sidebar shows the same raised play button as tiles.
- **Toolbar colours.** Text and icons in the toolbar are a step stronger so they read clearly on glass.

## Removed

- **Refresh button.** The sidebar's refresh button is gone. The library refreshes on its own.
